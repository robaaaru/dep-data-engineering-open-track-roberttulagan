Why look at both?

Typhoon warnings and rice prices tell two different parts of the story. Warnings show where serious storms were recorded. Rice prices show where prices moved up the most. Looking at both helps us see which places may need a closer look.

The simple question is: which Philippine regions and provinces had more serious warnings and bigger rice-price increases from 2024 to 2025?

Important framing

What this dashboard tells us

A highlighted province is a place worth checking more closely. It does not prove that a typhoon caused a price increase, damage, or hardship.

How to read the charts

How to read the charts

Serious warnings counts the storms where a province reached Wind Signal 3 or higher. Price rise shows the typical size of its upward price changes. Price drops do not add to this number.

In the first chart, the dashed lines mark the typical province. Points above both lines are labelled Most vulnerable. A point on a line is counted as lower.

Methodological boundary

A careful conclusion

This dashboard compares patterns. It does not explain why prices changed. Sulu and Batanes are not included in the price comparison because price readings are missing.
